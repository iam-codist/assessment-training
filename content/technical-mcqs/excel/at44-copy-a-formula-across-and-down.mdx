# Copy a formula across and down

## Question

Cell C2 contains =$A2*B$1. What formula results when it is copied one column right and one row down to D3?

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | =$A3*C$1 |
| B | =$B3*C$2 |
| C | =$A2*B$1 |
| D | =$A3*B$2 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

A. =$A3*C$1

## Explanation

The dollar sign before A fixes the column while row 2 moves to 3. B is a relative column and moves to C. The dollar sign before 1 fixes the row. Thus the formula is =$A3*C$1. Trace the row and column movement separately.

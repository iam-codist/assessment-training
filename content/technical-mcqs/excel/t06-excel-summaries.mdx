# Excel summaries

## Question

You have thousands of sales records with Region and Amount fields. Which feature most directly creates an interactive total by region without manually writing a formula for each region?

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | Page Break Preview |
| B | PivotTable |
| C | Spell Check |
| D | Slide Master |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

B. PivotTable

## Explanation

A PivotTable can group records by region and aggregate their amounts.

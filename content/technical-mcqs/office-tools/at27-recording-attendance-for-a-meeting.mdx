# Recording attendance for a meeting

## Question

A team needs to send an invitation with a time and track who accepts. Which Outlook item should they create?

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | Email signature |
| B | Contact group |
| C | Draft note |
| D | Calendar meeting invitation |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

D. Calendar meeting invitation

## Explanation

A meeting invitation specifies scheduling details and can track attendee responses.

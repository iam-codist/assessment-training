# Prioritizing two urgent requests

## Question

Two stakeholders ask for different tasks by the same time and you cannot complete both well. What is a sensible response?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Clarify impact and deadlines, make the capacity conflict visible and agree on priority with the responsible people.

## Explanation

Do not silently choose based only on who asks most loudly. Explain what can be done and what would move. Escalation is useful when you lack authority to decide; it is stronger when accompanied by options. Self-review: clarity, fair prioritization and realistic commitments. These are discussion frameworks; several well-reasoned responses can be appropriate. Answer personality questionnaires honestly and consistently.

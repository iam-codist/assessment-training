# An unfamiliar task and a learning plan

> Newly authored practice to complete the training roadmap. This is not a recovered chat question or an official assessment item.

## Question

You receive a task involving a tool you have not used before. How can you show initiative without overstating your ability?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Clarify the desired outcome, identify a small first experiment, consult reliable documentation and request targeted help when needed.

## Explanation

A credible plan names what you already know and what you must learn. Time-box exploration and share findings before a deadline is at risk. Honest learning is more useful than claiming expertise or waiting without action. Self-review: honest self-assessment, an actionable plan and communication. These are discussion frameworks; several well-reasoned responses can be appropriate. Answer personality questionnaires honestly and consistently.

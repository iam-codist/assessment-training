# Disagreeing during a code review

## Question

A teammate prefers an approach you believe will be harder to maintain. How do you disagree constructively?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

State the specific tradeoff, ask about their constraints and compare alternatives against shared criteria.

## Explanation

Discuss behavior and evidence rather than the person. For example, compare testability, complexity and delivery cost with a small example. Accept a reasoned team decision and document the tradeoff. Self-review: did you listen, explain evidence and help reach a decision? These are discussion frameworks; several well-reasoned responses can be appropriate. Answer personality questionnaires honestly and consistently.

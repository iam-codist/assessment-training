# Strengths and areas to improve

## Question

Prepare one strength and one area you are improving.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

For each, give a real example. For the improvement area, include the action you are taking and how you check progress.

## Explanation

Avoid a memorized “weakness” that is only disguised praise. Choose something you can discuss honestly and constructively, such as estimating work or explaining solutions concisely. An action and follow-up observation make the response credible. There is no single personality trait that every student must claim.

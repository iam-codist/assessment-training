# Explain your project with evidence

> Newly authored practice to complete the training roadmap. This is not a recovered chat question or an official assessment item.

## Question

How would you explain a student project in two minutes?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Cover the problem, users, your contribution, one design decision, how you checked it and what you would improve.

## Explanation

Use a real project and distinguish your own work from your teammates’ work. A small feature described clearly is stronger than a long list of technologies. Prepare one concrete example of input, processing and output, and be ready to show or explain a test. Do not claim usage or impact numbers you did not measure.

</details>

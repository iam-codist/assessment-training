# A clear sixty-second introduction

## Question

Prepare a 45–60 second introduction for a student interview using only truthful details.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Use four parts: your current studies, one relevant skill, a specific project or learning example, and what you want to contribute next.

## Explanation

A practice template is: “I am studying [course]. I have been building [skill] through [real example]. In that work, I [your contribution] and learned [lesson]. I am interested in [role] because [specific reason].” Replace every bracket with your own facts. Record yourself if you wish and review clarity, relevance and pace; this site does not record or score speech.

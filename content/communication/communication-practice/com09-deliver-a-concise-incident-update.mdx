# Deliver a concise incident update

## Question

Speak for 60–90 seconds using these facts. At 10:00 users reported checkout errors. Browsing works. Engineers are investigating a payment timeout. The cause is unconfirmed. The next update is at 10:30. Include impact, action and next update without claiming a fix.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

“Some customers are seeing checkout errors reported since 10:00. Product browsing is working. The engineering team is investigating payment timeouts; the underlying cause is still being checked. We will share the next update at 10:30, including any confirmed recovery estimate.”

## Explanation

Check for all five facts, a clear distinction between observation and hypothesis, and a specific follow-up time. Speak at a comfortable pace and listen to your recording once. Avoid filling the time with unsupported estimates.

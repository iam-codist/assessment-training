# Explain a technical idea to a beginner

## Question

Explain a queue in two or three sentences without assuming the listener knows data structures.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

“A queue is like a line at a service counter. New items join at the back, and the earliest item leaves from the front. We use this order when requests should be handled in arrival order.”

## Explanation

The analogy establishes the order, the operations make it precise and the example explains when it is useful. Avoid saying that every real service system is strictly first come first served; priority systems use different rules. Self-review: accuracy, familiar language and brevity.

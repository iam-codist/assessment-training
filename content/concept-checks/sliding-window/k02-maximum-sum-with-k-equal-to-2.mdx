# Maximum sum with K equal to 2

## Question

For nums = [1,4,2,7,3] and K = 2, what is the maximum sum?

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 9 |
| B | 10 |
| C | 7 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

B. 10

## Explanation

The window sums are 5, 6, 9 and 10. The best window is [7,3].

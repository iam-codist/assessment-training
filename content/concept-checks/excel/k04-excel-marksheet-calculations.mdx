# Excel marksheet calculations

## Question

C2:C6 contain 85, 90, the text Absent, 76, and 0. Evaluate COUNT(C2:C6), COUNTA(C2:C6), SUM(C2:C6), and COUNTIF(C2:C6,">=80").

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

COUNT = 4; COUNTA = 5; SUM = 251; COUNTIF = 2.

## Explanation

Zero is numeric. Absent is text but nonempty. Only 85 and 90 are at least 80.

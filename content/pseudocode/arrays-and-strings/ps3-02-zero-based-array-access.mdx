# Zero-based array access

## Question

A=[1,3,5,7]; s=A[1]+A[3]. What is s?

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 6 |
| B | 10 |
| C | 8 |
| D | 12 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

B. 10

## Explanation

Zero-based indices 1 and 3 hold 3 and 7.

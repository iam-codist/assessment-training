# Recursive doubling

## Question

F(0)=1; F(n)=2\*F(n-1) for n\>0. What is F(3)?

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 6 |
| B | 4 |
| C | 8 |
| D | 16 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

C. 8

## Explanation

Three doublings of 1 give 8.

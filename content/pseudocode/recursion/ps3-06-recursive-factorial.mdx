# Recursive factorial

## Question

F(1)=1; F(n)=n\*F(n-1) for n\>1. What is F(4)?

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 10 |
| B | 24 |
| C | 16 |
| D | 6 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

B. 24

## Explanation

The product is 4 times 3 times 2 times 1.

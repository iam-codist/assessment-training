# Bitwise AND + OR

## Question

What is printed by this pseudocode?

```text
INTEGER a = 6, b = 3

INTEGER x = a AND b
INTEGER y = a OR b

PRINT x + y
```

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 7 |
| B | 8 |
| C | 9 |
| D | 10 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

C. 9

## Explanation

6 AND 3 = 2; 6 OR 3 = 7; their sum is 9.

# The reptile circuit

## Question

Start at a corner and move clockwise around the outside, then finish in the centre. Fill the missing letters to name a large reptile found in Asia and Australia.

| Row / Col | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | C | ? | O |
| 2 | L | E | C |
| 3 | ? | D | ? |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

CROCODILE

## Explanation

The word is CROCODILE. Read the outer ring clockwise, then the centre.

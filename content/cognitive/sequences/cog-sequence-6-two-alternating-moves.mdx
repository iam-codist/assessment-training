# Two alternating moves

## Question

Find the missing number using the simplest consistent pattern. Explain the step between terms.

```text
4 → 8 → 6 → 12 → 10 → ? → 18
```

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 16 |
| B | 18 |
| C | 20 |
| D | 22 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

C. 20

## Explanation

Alternate ×2 and −2. After 10, multiply by 2 to get 20, then subtract 2 to get 18.

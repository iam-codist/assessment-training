# Interleaved patterns

## Question

Find the missing number using the simplest consistent pattern. Explain the step between terms.

```text
2 → 10 → 4 → 20 → 6 → ? → 8 → 40
```

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 12 |
| B | 24 |
| C | 30 |
| D | 32 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

C. 30

## Explanation

Odd positions are 2, 4, 6, 8. Even positions are 10, 20, 30, 40. The missing sixth term is 30.

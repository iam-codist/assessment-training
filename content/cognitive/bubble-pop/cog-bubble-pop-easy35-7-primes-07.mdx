## Question

Pop all PRIME numbers.

| Bubble | Expression / number |
| --- | --- |
| A | 13 |
| B | 29 |
| C | 37 |
| D | 19 |

## Answer

Select 13, 29, 37, 19. Any order is valid.

## Explanation

Correct selections: 13, 29, 37, 19.
Prime numbers have exactly two positive divisors: 1 and themselves. Select every match in any order; leave the other bubbles untouched.

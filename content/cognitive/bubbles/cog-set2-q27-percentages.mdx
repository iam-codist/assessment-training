# Percentages

## Question

Bubbles with the values shown float on the screen. Pop them in ASCENDING order of value. Which bubble is popped 3rd?

| Bubble | Expression / number |
| --- | --- |
| A | 30% of 90 |
| B | 20% of 150 |
| C | 45% of 40 |
| D | 12% of 200 |
| E | 55% of 60 |

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 30% of 90 |
| B | 55% of 60 |
| C | 20% of 150 |
| D | 45% of 40 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

A. 30% of 90

## Explanation

Convert every bubble to a plain number first.

Sort the values in ascending (smallest first) order.

The answer is 30% of 90.

| Expression / number | Value |
| --- | --- |
| 30% of 90 | 27 |
| 20% of 150 | 30 |
| 45% of 40 | 18 |
| 12% of 200 | 24 |
| 55% of 60 | 33 |

**Eligible pop order:** 45% of 40 → 12% of 200 → 30% of 90 → 20% of 150 → 55% of 60.

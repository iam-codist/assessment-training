# Percentages

## Question

Bubbles with the values shown float on the screen. Pop them in DESCENDING order of value. Which bubble is popped 2nd?

| Bubble | Expression / number |
| --- | --- |
| A | 25% of 80 |
| B | 15% of 200 |
| C | 40% of 45 |
| D | 60% of 35 |
| E | 10% of 250 |

## Options

| Option | Answer choice |
| --- | --- |
| A | 25% of 80 |
| B | 10% of 250 |
| C | 40% of 45 |
| D | 60% of 35 |

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

B. 10% of 250

## Explanation

Convert every bubble to a plain number first.

Sort the values in descending (largest first) order.

The answer is 10% of 250.

| Expression / number | Value |
| --- | --- |
| 25% of 80 | 20 |
| 15% of 200 | 30 |
| 40% of 45 | 18 |
| 60% of 35 | 21 |
| 10% of 250 | 25 |

**Eligible pop order:** 15% of 200 → 10% of 250 → 60% of 35 → 25% of 80 → 40% of 45.

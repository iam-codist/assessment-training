# Source and attribution notes

The initial collection was organized on 15 November 2026 from assessment-training discussions and the teaching material created or supplied in those sessions. Source context is retained on every question without publishing private conversation links or raw chat exports.

| Material | Treatment in this repository |
| --- | --- |
| Original placement practice pack | 23 technical MCQs, 22 pseudocode exercises and 20 coding problems; original teaching explanations retained |
| Pseudocode sessions | 20 medium questions and additional worked examples, with conventions made explicit |
| Day 1, Day 2 and demonstration lessons | Related coding discussions consolidated; differing variants kept separately |
| Technical demonstration slides | Five MCQs, two pseudocode examples and the Excel concept example |
| Cognitive classroom material | 225 prompts with text boards or reference images, available answer keys and review status |
| Historical blog summary | 20 brief outlines; these are not presented as complete solved questions |

The November 2026 curriculum expansion adds **57 newly authored exercises**: 8 CS fundamentals, 3 dynamic programming, 10 SQL, 12 frontend, 6 behavioral, 8 communication and 10 interview prompts. These are visibly labelled and do not claim to have been recovered from chat histories. New runnable references in C, C++, Python and Java cover all 49 complete coding lessons; the original classroom variants and source notes remain intact.

`LC` IDs refer to the corresponding problems on [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problemset/). Statements here are concise teaching paraphrases; associated names and platform rights remain with their owners. Some mapped classroom variants use different rules.

Cognitive source filenames are recorded on their question pages. Image-only prompts with no verified answer key remain marked `needs-review`. Original documents and private transcripts are not part of this public repository. The Cognitive Lab application source and the image prompts it needs are included for the requested website integration.

No blanket open-source licence is applied to mixed-origin teaching content or third-party images. Public access is provided for study; maintainers should confirm reuse rights before adding a repository-wide licence or redistributing third-party assets separately.

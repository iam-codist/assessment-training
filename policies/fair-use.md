# Fair Use and Third-Party Material

Assessment Training uses source references to help readers trace teaching examples and verify explanations. Third-party items may be linked, used under permission or a license, or included where applicable law permits. Copyright exceptions vary by jurisdiction and depend on the circumstances. An educational purpose or a source credit alone does not establish permission to reproduce a work.

Project licenses cover only the original material identified in [Attribution and Credits](attribution.md). Before reusing a third-party item, check its original source and follow its license or obtain permission where required.

If you own material featured here and believe a credit or use needs correction, contact the maintainer through the [repository](https://github.com/iam-codist/assessment-training). Include the affected page or question ID, the original source and the change you are requesting. Keep sensitive information out of public issues.

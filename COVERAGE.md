## How to use the Cognitive Lab

Choose an activity, read the instructions and press Play when ready. Submit your answer to reveal feedback. Use Pause, Hold for discussion or manual pacing when practising with a class. You can also use the same controls for solo practice.

The lab contains 13 formats and 225 prompts from the classroom material already represented in the reading library. These are two ways to practise the same collection, not 225 additional unique questions. Session data stays in the current tab’s memory. Export the CSV report before refreshing or closing; there are no student accounts or saved progress database.

Visual prompts without a verified answer remain discussion activities. The inconsistent Geo-Sudo clue set remains flagged for review. Practice scores are learning feedback, not a validated measure of cognitive ability or an employer’s hiring score.

## What changed in this expansion

Previously missing areas now include newly authored lessons and worked references. These are labelled **Newly authored practice** so they are not mistaken for questions recovered from past chats. The two planned sliding-window lessons now include new worked examples and four-language implementations.

Historical gaps remain visible: 20 incomplete outlines, 11 visual prompts without verified keys and the inconsistent Geo-Sudo puzzle. See [known gaps](KNOWN-GAPS.md). Behavioral and interview prompts use response frameworks because several answers may be appropriate; they are not personality answer keys. Communication practice includes text and partner-led exercises, not automated speech scoring.

# Decimal and binary conversion practice

## Question

Convert 13, 25 and 14 from decimal to binary.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

13 → 1101; 25 → 11001; 14 → 1110.

## Explanation

Add powers of two, or divide repeatedly by two and read the remainders bottom to top.

# Grammar and subject–verb agreement

## Question

Choose the correct sentence.

A. Each of the students have a login.
B. Each of the students has a login.
C. Each of the students having a login.
D. Each of the students were a login.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

B. Each of the students has a login.

## Explanation

The grammatical subject is “Each,” which is singular, so use “has.” The plural noun “students” appears inside the prepositional phrase “of the students” and does not control the verb here.

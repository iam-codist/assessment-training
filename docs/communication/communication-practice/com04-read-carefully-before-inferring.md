# Read carefully before inferring

> Newly authored practice to complete the training roadmap. This is not a recovered chat question or an official assessment item.

## Question

Read: “The workshop starts at 10:00. Registration opens at 9:30 and closes at 9:50. Bring a laptop; loan devices are not available.”

When does registration close, and can a learner rely on borrowing a laptop at the venue?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Registration closes at 9:50. No: the text says loan devices are not available.

## Explanation

Use the passage’s explicit details. Do not confuse the workshop start time with registration closing time. The passage does not say that late entry is allowed or that arriving without a laptop cancels attendance; those would be unsupported additions.

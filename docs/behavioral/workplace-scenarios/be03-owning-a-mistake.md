# Owning a mistake

> Newly authored practice to complete the training roadmap. This is not a recovered chat question or an official assessment item.

## Question

Your change caused a defect that a colleague discovered. Describe your immediate actions and follow-up.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Acknowledge your part, help assess impact, coordinate a safe correction and explain how recurrence will be reduced.

## Explanation

Avoid hiding the problem or blaming the tester. Confirm the scope before promising a fix, add a meaningful regression check and share what you learned. Use a real incident when answering an interview; do not invent production experience. Self-review: ownership, impact awareness and learning. These are discussion frameworks; several well-reasoned responses can be appropriate. Answer personality questionnaires honestly and consistently.

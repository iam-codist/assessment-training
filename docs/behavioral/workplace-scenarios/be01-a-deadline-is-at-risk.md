# A deadline is at risk

> Newly authored practice to complete the training roadmap. This is not a recovered chat question or an official assessment item.

## Question

You promised a feature for tomorrow but discover a dependency will take two more days. What would you do and what would you tell the team?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Flag the risk early, describe the blocked work, propose options and agree on the next update.

## Explanation

A useful response separates facts from estimates: explain what is complete, what is blocked and the consequence. Offer a smaller safe delivery or a revised date without making a promise you cannot support. Score your practice from 0–2 for early communication, ownership and a practical next step; there is no universal personality score. These are discussion frameworks; several well-reasoned responses can be appropriate. Answer personality questionnaires honestly and consistently.

# Responding to feedback

> Newly authored practice to complete the training roadmap. This is not a recovered chat question or an official assessment item.

## Question

A trainer says your solutions work but your explanations are hard to follow. How would you respond?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Ask for a concrete example, restate the feedback and practise a shorter explanation with a dry run.

## Explanation

Turn broad feedback into observable behavior. Explain input, approach, one example and complexity, then request follow-up feedback. You can disagree with part of the feedback while still checking what the listener experienced. Self-review: openness, specificity and follow-through. These are discussion frameworks; several well-reasoned responses can be appropriate. Answer personality questionnaires honestly and consistently.

# Handle a release risk under deadline pressure

## Question

A teammate finds a defect that could expose invoices to another customer. The release is due in 30 minutes and your lead is unavailable. Explain what you would do, who you would contact and how you would communicate the impact.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Pause the affected release step within your authority, record the reproducible defect without exposing customer data, contact the designated backup owner or incident channel, and propose a safe release scope or delay with a clear next update time.

## Explanation

A strong answer identifies customer impact, takes ownership, uses escalation routes and states what evidence is available. Explain authority limits and reversible actions. Evaluate the response by its reasoning and communication. Use truthful examples when reflecting on your own experience.

</details>

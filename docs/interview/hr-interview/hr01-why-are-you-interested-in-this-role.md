# Why are you interested in this role

> Newly authored practice to complete the training roadmap. This is not a recovered chat question or an official assessment item.

## Question

How can you answer “Why this role?” without relying on a generic company slogan?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

Connect a real responsibility in the job description to your skills, one example of evidence and a realistic learning goal.

## Explanation

Research the current role before the interview. Explain what interests you specifically, such as building reliable interfaces or solving customer problems. Do not invent company projects or claim you know an internal team. A strong answer is specific while remaining truthful.

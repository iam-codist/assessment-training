# A conflict using STAR

## Question

Describe a disagreement using Situation, Task, Action and Result.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

State the context briefly, identify your responsibility, explain what you did and describe the outcome or lesson.

## Explanation

Use a real event from study, volunteering or work. Spend most of the time on your actions and reasoning. Give credit to others and avoid personal blame. If the result was mixed, say so and describe what you would change. STAR is a communication structure, not permission to fabricate an achievement.

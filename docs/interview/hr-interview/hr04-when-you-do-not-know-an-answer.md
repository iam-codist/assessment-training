# When you do not know an answer

> Newly authored practice to complete the training roadmap. This is not a recovered chat question or an official assessment item.

## Question

An interviewer asks about a topic you have not studied. How do you respond?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

## Answer

State the limit honestly, share relevant knowledge if useful and explain how you would investigate the question.

## Explanation

For a problem-solving question, ask whether you may work through it aloud. Distinguish a hypothesis from a fact. A concise “I have not used that tool yet; here is how I would learn or verify it” is more credible than confident guessing. Follow-up learning can be part of your preparation plan.
